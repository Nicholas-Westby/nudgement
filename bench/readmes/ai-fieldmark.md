# 🌴 Fieldmark

### Your Ultimate AI-Powered Survey Planning Companion ✈️

---

## 🌍 Welcome to Fieldmark!

Planning a survey should be exciting, not exhausting. That's why we built **Fieldmark**, a beautifully crafted native macOS application that transforms the way you plan, organize and experience your outings.

Whether you're a solo backpacker exploring hidden gems, a family planning the perfect getaway, or a group of friends coordinating an epic adventure, Fieldmark has everything you need to turn your visit dreams into reality.

## ✨ Why Fieldmark?

- 🧠 **AI-Powered Intelligence**: Let cutting-edge AI do the heavy lifting of outing research
- 🗺️ **Stunning Visualizations**: See your outing come alive on beautiful interactive maps
- 🤝 **Seamless Collaboration**: Plan together with friends and family in real time
- 🔒 **Privacy First**: Your data stays on your Mac, stored as simple files you own
- ⚡ **Lightning Fast**: Built natively with SwiftUI for buttery-smooth performance

## 🚀 Features

### 🤖 AI Outing Planning

Simply describe your dream outing, and Fieldmark's powerful AI engine will research the region, discover amazing sites and craft a personalized day-by-day route tailored just for you.

### 📍 Sites of Interest

Effortlessly collect and organize sites of interest with names, notes, links, addresses and images. Fieldmark makes it easy to keep all your visit inspiration in one site.

### 🗺️ Interactive Map View

Visualize your entire outing on a gorgeous interactive map powered by MapKit. Color-coded pins make it easy to see everything at a glance.

### 🖼️ Gallery View

Browse your sites in a stunning visual gallery that brings your destinations to life.

### 🔍 Smart Discovery

Discover hidden gems near any location with intelligent recommendations that filter out chains and highlight authentic local experiences.

### ✨ Automatic Annotation

Fieldmark automatically annotates your sites with summaries, photos, review scores and more, saving you hours of research.

### 👥 Real-Time Sharing

Share your survey with visit companions and collaborate seamlessly. Changes sync automatically, so everyone stays on the same page.

### 📝 Field Notes

Keep all your important outing information in one beautiful rich-text document that the whole group can edit together.

### 📅 Smart Route

Optimize your route with a single click and let Fieldmark plan your days for maximum enjoyment and minimum visit time.

## 🎯 Getting Started

Getting started with Fieldmark is easy! Simply create your first survey, add a few sites you'd love to visit, and let Fieldmark take care of the rest. In just a few minutes, you'll have a beautifully organized outing ready to go.

## 💎 What Makes Fieldmark Different?

Unlike other visit planning apps, Fieldmark combines the power of artificial intelligence with the simplicity of plain files. Your data is always yours, stored in human-readable formats on your own Mac. And with its intelligent AI features, Fieldmark takes the guesswork out of visit planning, so you can focus on what really matters: making memories.
